# Request to JULAC for pre-2017 Hong Kong bibliographic data

Why this exists: the Hong Kong catalogue on the site comes from the Books
Registration Office feed on DATA.GOV.HK, which begins at 2017 Q1 — 2016 and
earlier return 404 under every URL form, and the registry lists nothing
earlier, because the registration programme itself does not go back further.
Open Library fills part of the gap but 96% of its Hong Kong records carry
romanised rather than Chinese titles, so a reader searching 香港 cannot find
them.

HKALL holds over five million monograph titles across the eight UGC-funded
university libraries, and JULAC describes it as the first such project to
include a large number of Chinese vernacular items — which is exactly what is
missing. It cannot be harvested: it runs on Ex Libris Alma behind a login, its
robots.txt disallows the search paths, and it is a service for the students and
staff of the eight institutions. So the way to get it is to ask.

The licensing question is the real obstacle rather than the access one. Alma
bibliographic records are commonly OCLC-derived, and OCLC's terms restrict
redistribution. The draft raises this rather than waiting to be told, and offers
to accept a narrower arrangement.

## Draft (English)

**Subject: Enquiry about bibliographic data for pre-2017 Hong Kong imprints**

Dear Ms Ko,

I maintain a small non-commercial open-data project, 神保町 (jimbocho.app), which
turns Hong Kong and Taiwan book metadata into a browsable, searchable catalogue.
It is built from official open data: the Books Registration Office's catalogue of
books printed in Hong Kong, published on DATA.GOV.HK, and the National Central
Library's new-book feed in Taiwan.

The Hong Kong side has a hard limit. The registration catalogue begins at 2017
Q1, so the site currently holds no Hong Kong imprint published before 2017. The
openly licensed alternatives are thin: Open Library has some Hong Kong editions,
but the great majority carry romanised rather than Chinese titles, which makes
them effectively unsearchable for a Chinese-reading audience.

HKALL is the obvious place where that earlier material is properly catalogued,
and I understand it is a service for the eight institutions' students and staff
rather than a public data source, so I am writing to ask rather than to collect.

My question is whether JULAC would consider sharing bibliographic records for
Hong Kong imprints published before 2017 — a filtered extract rather than the
whole union catalogue, and a modest set of fields would be enough: title
(including the Chinese title as catalogued), author, publisher, place and year of
publication, ISBN where present, and a classification or subject heading.

I am conscious that the licensing of the underlying records may be the real
constraint, since records in a shared ILS are often derived from OCLC or other
vendors whose terms restrict redistribution. If that rules out sharing the
records themselves, I would welcome any of the following instead:

- a subset limited to records the member libraries originally created, rather
  than vendor-sourced copy;
- a reduced field set, if fuller records cannot be redistributed;
- a pointer to whoever at a member library holds the Hong Kong Collection
  metadata, if that is a more appropriate route than HKALL as a whole;
- or simply confirmation that this is not possible, which is useful to know.

If data were shared, the project would credit JULAC and the contributing
libraries on the dataset's own page, state the provenance plainly alongside the
official registry datasets, and honour any attribution, field or
non-redistribution conditions attached. The site is non-commercial and carries
no advertising.

I am glad to describe the project in more detail, or to adjust the request to
whatever shape is workable.

With thanks for your time,

(your name)
(your email / contact)
